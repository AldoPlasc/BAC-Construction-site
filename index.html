How It Works

Our Process

No surprises. Here's exactly what happens from your first call to the final walkthrough.

01

Let's Talk

Tell us what you're looking to build or remodel, and we'll set up a time to walk the property.

02

Site Visit

We visit in person to understand the scope, constraints, and what's actually involved.

03

Proposal

You get a clear, itemized estimate — with any added costs flagged upfront, not after the fact.

04

Build

Our crew handles construction on-site daily, with regular updates so you're never left wondering.

05

Final Walkthrough

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get in contact with you?

Call us directly at (650) 391-8498, or fill out the estimate request form below and our team will follow up to schedule a free consultation.

How do you determine pricing for your services?

Every project starts with a free, itemized estimate based on scope, materials, and timeline. If anything unexpected comes up mid-project — common in older Bay Area homes — we flag the added cost before doing the work, never after.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the greater Bay Area, including San Jose, San Mateo, Fremont, Mountain View, and surrounding communities.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — B.A.C Construction LLC is fully licensed and bonded.

How long will my project take?

Timeline depends on project size and scope, and we'll give you a clear estimate upfront during your free consultation.

Do you handle permits?

Yes, we handle permitting as part of the project scope for jobs that require it.

Do you work with architects or designers?

Yes — we can work alongside your architect or designer, or help coordinate that piece for you.

Can you help with material selection?

Yes, our team can guide you through material and finish choices as part of the design phase.

Do you work on occupied homes?

Yes — we regularly work on occupied homes and take care to keep the site clean and livable throughout.

What size projects do you take on?

From single-room remodels to full custom home builds — reach out with your project and we'll let you know if it's a fit.
